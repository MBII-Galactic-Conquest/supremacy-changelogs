01/07/2025 - v1.0.1 changelogs:

- Siegeteam fixes for Lego Star Wars eras.
- gfx/sup_icon fixes for a several misplaced or mishandled icons.
- ForceInfo additions, icon & sound replacements for protect/absorb for certain classes.
>> sound/forcesup/protect.mp3 & protectloop.wav
> Protect == Officer Shroud // gfx/mp/sup_ofc_shroud.tga
> Absorb == Nullify Force // gfx/mp/sup_vet_nullify.tga
> These abilities use MB_ATT_LIGHTS_BEACON.

- Incremental movement speed adjustments to a few classes, namely Veteran/Vanguard & Imperial Shock trooper.
- Default soldier droid HP reduced by 5 points for overall balance, for Phase I & Phase II units. Relying more upon armor, and group tactics.
- Some eras now have officer & veteran abilities for their units, that previously missed them in v1.0:
> Zakuul Knight can now use stronger variant of Officer Shroud, given protect 3.
> Veteran Skytrooper may now use Nullify Force, paired with their jetpack fuel.
> Dathomiir Zombie Officer may now use Officer Shroud, in undead survival eras.
> Vong Head Grunt can now use Officer Shroud, and Nullify Force both.

- Blaster VFX fixes for certain clone wars classes.
- Jetpack wrist rocket SBD animation fixes.
- Other minor bugfixes and optimizations.

- Rock the Era & Rock the Bonus has extensive overhaul and changes to the backend, better serving the player experience.
