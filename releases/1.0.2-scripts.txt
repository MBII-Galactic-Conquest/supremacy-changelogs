Rock the Era systems for the MBII Supremacy servers now feature the credit purchase points database & reinforcements tickets system:

** A gamemode where the gameplay focus is finally on more than the round based objectives themselves. **

The opus of play is now centered on bettering your team. Players come and go, and overall contribute to a constant tug of war, empowering your team by wisely spending your teams balance on stronger classes to sharpen your edge against the enemy team.

- !points or !pts = view both teams credits, global
- !tickets or !tkts = view both teams reinforcements remaining, global

C2LWTnk.png

Average of 3-4 points are given per kill for your team.
All reinforcements tickets and points are automatically shown after 10 kills.
All teams start with 500 credits per era vote, and 50 reinforcements tickets per map.
Enemy Team depleted of all their tickets generate 100 credits for the winning team, 50 for the losing team.
Either team depleted of all their tickets generate a Rock the Bonus vote, which may be followed up by Rock the Vote.
Garrison Bonus provides +15 extra reinforcements tickets per map for your team.

Vote !6 to abstain in bonus voting if your team wishes to preserve their credits, or simply don't vote.
Default siegeteams are provided when your team cannot afford the below bonuses.
