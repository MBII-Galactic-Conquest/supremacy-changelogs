The following combat bonuses will be available for Supremacy:



Two defensive bonuses may be paired.

Only one offensive bonus may be used.



Using an offensive bonus prevents use of defensive ones, & vice versa.



- Supplies bonus (D) - all soldiers have +100 ammo, +50 magazine, +1 frag, access to engineer class.

- Bacta/Kolto bonus (D) - all soldiers have passive health regen, +10hp, a small bacta tank, access to medic class.

- Garrison bonus (D) - allows special units in the match, soldier lives increased by +1

- Combat Shielding bonus (D) - allows all soldiers to purchase shield recharge, +10ar.

- Energy Boost bonus (D) - allows all soldiers the ability to arc roll & use stamina. kotor eras get vibrosword defense.

- Enhanced Blasters bonus (O) - gives soldiers a small percentile increase to all damage (5%), pistol 3, and e-11 3 accuracy.

- Generic Leader bonus (O) - allows stronger generic leaders in the match, such as heroes, bounty hunters, jedi/sith duelists & sorcs.

- Named Leader bonus (O) - used to bring extremely powerful, named leaders from the Star Wars universe into the fray.

- MVP bonus (O) - used in accordance with the later planned scrim Galactic Conquest & Campaign aspects of the gamemode.



Defensive ones may be combined, sharing their bonuses in the following .mbch format:



- bg - Bacta/Kolto & Garrison

- bs - Bacta/Kolto & Supplies

- bc - Bacta/Kolto & Combat Shielding

- sg - Supplies & Garrison

- cg - Combat Shielding & Garrison

- cs - Combat Shielding & Supplies

- eng - Energy Boost & Garrison

- ens - Energy Boost & Supplies

- enb - Energy Boost & Bacta/Kolto

- enc - Energy Boost & Combat Shielding





Class Specific Highlights & Synergies:



- Veterans/Vanguards can now dash [CS1], and use rage points for team absorb.

- Veterans/Vanguards may choose between T21 or A280, skinned as their era specific weapon.

- Officers can now dash [CS1]. As well as use rage points for dodge [CS2] and team protect.
They also now have fast firing pistol 3, and a lot of gadgets to compensate for one life.

- Medic Class can now use rage points to Team Heal, as well as flip kick.

- Engineer Class can now use rage points to Team Energize.

- Jetpack troopers, all with era specific models, thanks to our modeling team!

- All default soldier classes, impacted by all bonuses, have point buy to interact with these systems.





The point of these class synergies is to introduce a new way of tactics and gameplay to ordinary gunner play. While some eras introduce weaker Jedi and Sith into the field, the emphasis is on gunner play now more than ever, with interesting ways to bring them together. The war of the generic soldier class, from open, but intricately better!
